## How to use

1. Generate each image at **1536 × 1024 px (3:2 landscape)** unless the row says
   otherwise.
2. Paste the **subject line**, then the **style block** underneath it. The style
   block is the same every time — that is what keeps the set looking like one
   company.
3. Save with the file name in the table. Send everything in one folder.

### Style block — paste this after every subject line

```
Photorealistic documentary photograph of a real Indian industrial site.
Workers wear royal-blue work shirts with reflective silver bands, navy
trousers, blue or yellow hard hats, safety shoes and white gloves. The Aqua
Facility Services mark — a golden letter A with maroon lettering — appears
small and natural on uniforms, signage, trolleys or crates, never as a large
logo. Bright, clean industrial lighting, natural colours, believable depth of
field, sharp focus. Candid working moment, not a posed studio shot. No text
overlays other than ordinary site signage, no watermark, no distorted hands or
faces. Wide 3:2 landscape framing.
```
