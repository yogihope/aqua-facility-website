## 1. industry-heavy-engineering.jpg — 1536 × 1024 px

Photorealistic documentary photograph of a real Indian heavy engineering works. A cleaning crew is clearing and sweeping around a large fabrication bay while an overhead crane carries a steel structure above them. Workers wear royal-blue work shirts with reflective silver bands, navy trousers, blue or yellow hard hats, safety shoes and white gloves. The Aqua Facility Services mark — a golden letter A with maroon lettering — appears small and natural on uniforms, signage or a cleaning trolley, never as a large logo. Bright, clean industrial lighting, natural colours, believable depth of field, sharp focus. A candid working moment, not a posed studio shot. No text overlays other than ordinary site signage, no watermark, no distorted hands or faces. Wide 3:2 landscape framing.
