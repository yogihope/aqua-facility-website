## Rules for every photo

- **Orientation:** landscape (wide), unless the table says portrait.
- **Size:** at least 2000px on the long edge. Straight from the camera or phone
  is fine — do not crop or compress before sending.
- **People:** staff in proper uniform, with helmet, gloves and shoes as the site
  requires. A photo showing someone without required PPE cannot go on the site.
- **Permission:** anyone whose face is clearly visible must be okay with the
  photo being public. For client sites, get the client's permission first.
- **Client identity:** no client name, logo, board or vehicle number in frame
  unless that client has approved being named publicly.
- **Light:** daylight or bright site light. Avoid dark, blurry or heavily
  filtered shots.
- **No stock photos.** Real Aqua sites, real Aqua people.

**Where to send them:** put the files in a folder and share it. I will rename,
crop and place them. Do not rename them yourself — just tell me which page each
one is for.
